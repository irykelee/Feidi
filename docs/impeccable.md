## Design Context

### Users
People using a desktop computer and one or more nearby phones or PCs on the same LAN to transfer text, images, and files quickly without an external service.

### Brand Personality
Reserved, tool-like, privacy-first, and trustworthy. The interface should help users complete a transfer with minimal distraction and clear status feedback.

### Aesthetic Direction
A restrained utility interface: clear hierarchy, compact but readable information, strong state visibility, and no decorative effects that compete with transfer status. Support both light and dark themes with WCAG 2.2 AA as the accessibility baseline.

### Design Principles
- Make connection, identity, transfer progress, and failure states immediately legible.
- Prefer direct actions and progressive disclosure over decorative UI.
- Preserve privacy cues without implying stronger security than the protocol provides.
- Keep touch, keyboard, focus, and reduced-motion behavior usable across PC and mobile clients.
